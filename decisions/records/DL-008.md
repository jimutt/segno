## Context

"MuseScore" is a trademark of its owners, and Segno is not affiliated with MuseScore. The repository is public, and every test score or demo score in it is redistributed with it.

## Decision

- **Names:** "MuseScore" may appear in descriptions ("for MuseScore exports", "MuseScore's position files"), but not in the names of the package, the element, the repository, the CLI or its commands.
- **Scores:** fixtures and demo scores must be in the public domain or under an open licence. Each one is listed in `fixtures/SOURCES.md` with its files, the score, its source and its licence, and carries whatever attribution the licence requires.
- **Exports from the original score:** fixtures are exported from the original score file (`.mscz`), not through a MusicXML round trip. A MusicXML round trip of one test score changed its endings (they came back numbered 1 and 3), and a re-export from it stopped half way through the second pass.

The first fixture is *När juldagsmorgon glimmar* from Fria Noter (frianoter.se), whose engravings are published under CC BY 4.0. The fixture files are its `.mpos` and `.spos` exports from MuseScore 4.7: one page, two staves, a pickup bar, and a repeat with 1st and 2nd endings.

## Rationale

Using a trademark in a name suggests endorsement; using it to describe compatibility does not. Open-licensed fixtures keep the repository redistributable, and listing their sources lets anyone check and reproduce them.

## Consequences

- New fixtures need a licence check and a `SOURCES.md` row in the same pull request.
- Scores under CC BY must keep their attribution wherever they are shown, including the demo site.
