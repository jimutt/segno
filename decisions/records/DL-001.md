## Context

Web notation libraries such as OSMD, Verovio and alphaTab draw the score themselves from MusicXML or similar, reflowing it to the screen. That sometimes makes a carefully engraved score harder to read. Where they offer playback, it is synthesized (alphaTab's built-in synthesizer, OSMD's separate audio add-on, or an app pairing Verovio's MIDI output with a synthesizer), or, in alphaTab's case, a recording aligned through sync points made by hand.

MuseScore can export, from one score, its engraved pages as SVG, its own audio rendering, and the position and start time of every bar and note in playback order (see DL-002). Together these are everything a player needs to follow the audio on the original pages, with no layout or alignment work of its own.

## Decision

Segno is a player that plays a score's own audio on its own engraved pages and follows along: a marker on the bar being played and a cursor that flows from note to note.

In scope:

- a dependency-free core library that parses MuseScore's position files, builds a compact sync map and looks up what plays at a given time (DL-003, DL-004);
- `<segno-player>`, a framework-free web component;
- a small CLI to build sync maps at build time, documentation for the MuseScore export, and a demo site.

Out of scope: editing, transposing, rendering MusicXML, and converting files. A hosted converter (send a score file, get the player's inputs back) may become a separate project later. Segno must not depend on one, but its sync map format should be easy for such a service to produce.

## Rationale

The engraver's layout is the best rendering of the score there is, and the score's own audio export is already aligned with it. A player that only displays and follows can stay small and exact: it never has to understand notation, repeats or tempo.

## Consequences

- Segno needs the exports of the score file, so it suits people who have the source score (publishers of their own editions, choirs, teachers), not arbitrary MusicXML.
- The pages are fixed images: on a narrow phone screen they don't reflow. A site that wants reflow can offer a notation library alongside Segno.
- Feature requests for rendering, editing or conversion are declined, or pointed at a separate project.
