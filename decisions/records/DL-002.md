## Context

MuseScore writes measure positions (`.mpos`) and segment positions (`.spos`) with its positions writer (`src/notation/internal/positionswriter.cpp` in the MuseScore repository). Both are small, flat XML files:

```xml
<score>
  <elements>
    <element id="0" x="13464.5" y="26393.2" sx="16788.6" sy="24486.9" page="0"/>
  </elements>
  <events>
    <event elid="0" position="0"/>
    <event elid="1" position="652"/>
  </events>
</score>
```

- `.mpos`: one element per bar. x/y is the bar's top-left on its page, sx the bar's width, sy the height of the whole system (all staves), page the zero-based page index.
- `.spos`: one element per segment with notes or rests (all voices and staves). x is where its noteheads start, sx its widest element, y/sy the system.
- events: the elements in playback order, with their start time in milliseconds.

Verified with MuseScore 4.7.5 (Linux build, headless):

- Repeats, voltas, D.C./D.S., codas and fine come out in playback order: the writer walks MuseScore's own unrolled repeat list. All 69 scores of MuseScore's repeat test suite (`src/engraving/tests/repeat_data`) matched the expected bar order. (8 of them look different at first sight only because the suite compares displayed bar numbers, which section breaks restart, while the positions use bar indices.)
- Times follow MuseScore's tempo map, ritardandos included.
- Coordinates are the SVG's own units × 12 at MuseScore's default PNG resolution: the writer scales by `pngDpi / DPI × 12`, and the default PNG resolution equals `DPI`. Overlaying the boxes on the SVG export puts each one exactly on its bar.
- The exported MP3 sounds a constant ~60 ms after the event times (synthesizer latency), with no drift, measured with onset detection against a click track over a 108 s score with repeats and a ritardando.
- The same writer feeds MuseScore's `--score-media` export, and it has been maintained through 2025–26; between 4.6.5 and 4.7.5 it only changed in plumbing.

## Decision

Segno's inputs are, all from one MuseScore export of one score:

- the `.mpos` file (required): bar boxes and the playback order with times;
- the `.spos` file (optional): note positions for the note cursor;
- the SVG pages, of which the core only needs each page's size (its `viewBox`, read by `readViewBox`);
- the audio.

Segno never interprets the score. MuseScore's unrolled event list is the truth for playback order and timing; Segno has no repeat, jump or tempo logic of its own.

Positions are converted with a fixed 12 position units per SVG unit (`MPOS_UNITS_PER_SVG_UNIT`), overridable through `buildSync`'s `unitsPerSvgUnit` option.

`buildSync` rejects files that don't belong together rather than drawing wrong markers. It throws when:

- the text has no elements, or an element has a non-numeric coordinate or page (not a positions file);
- an element is on a page that wasn't given;
- a box falls outside its page by more than 1% of the page (wrong units, another PNG resolution, or another score's pages);
- an event names an element that doesn't exist;
- event times go backwards.

The files are parsed with a small attribute parser (regular expressions over the `<element>` and `<event>` tags), not an XML library: the format is flat, and this keeps the core dependency-free and DOM-free (DL-004).

## Rationale

Computing playback order from the notation (MusicXML repeats, voltas, D.C./D.S. al Coda/Fine, jumps inside endings) is exactly the hard part that MuseScore's own test suite exists for; reusing MuseScore's result avoids reimplementing it and getting edge cases wrong. Aligning a recording to the score by hand or by onset detection is laborious or fragile; MuseScore's own audio is already aligned to its own events.

## Consequences

- Two export settings must be right, and the documentation has to say so: "Play repeats" must be on (the audio follows the setting, the positions always unroll repeats), and the PNG resolution must stay at its default (or the ×12 factor changes). The checks above catch a wrong resolution; a player must catch a missing repeat by checking that the audio is not shorter than the last event's time.
- A segment's `sx` is its widest element (accidentals, clusters), so the note cursor's x needs an estimate (DL-009).
- Segno depends on MuseScore keeping this writer. It backs MuseScore's own `--score-media` export and has been maintained, which makes that a reasonable bet.
- Other engravers could be supported later by producing a sync map (DL-003) from their own data, without changing the player.
