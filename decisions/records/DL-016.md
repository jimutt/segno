## Context

DL-005 settled that the built-in controls appear only with the `controls` attribute, as with `<audio>`, and that a site may bring its own controls in a slot.

## Decision

The built-in controls are a sticky bar of native form elements, following the layout of Fria Noter's shared `PlayerBar`:

- a play/pause `<button>` (part `play-button`) with an icon and an accessible name that follows the state;
- a back-to-beginning `<button>` (part `restart-button`) that pauses and seeks to zero;
- the elapsed time and duration on either side of the seek slider (both part `time`, individually `current-time` and `duration`);
- a seek `<input type="range">` over the whole audio (part `seek`), with an accessible value text ("0:12 of 0:28");
- a tempo stepper, 50–150% in steps of 10%, setting `playbackRate` with pitch preserved: native slower/faster buttons around a politely announced percentage (parts `tempo`, `tempo-decrease`, `tempo-value`, `tempo-increase`). The buttons disable at the limits; a rate set through the API is displayed accurately even between steps;
- a "Follow score" checkbox (parts `follow`, `follow-input`) that controls the boolean `follow` attribute. The checkbox stays in sync with attribute and property changes.

They render as the fallback content of `<slot name="controls">`, shown only when the host has `controls`. A site's own controls go in that slot and call the element's API. In a player narrower than 44rem (size container query), play, restart, elapsed time, seek and duration remain on one row; a settings button (part `settings-button`, with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`) reveals tempo and follow in a second row (part `settings`). Escape closes that panel and returns focus to the settings button. Above the breakpoint, tempo and follow are always visible. `--segno-controls: compact` shows only the play button and the seek slider, on one line (DL-015). The controls area is sticky at the bottom of the viewport, so the controls stay in reach while the pages scroll. Play, restart and seek are disabled until the audio has a usable duration.

**Keyboard**, on the focusable pages area: Space or K plays and pauses, ← and → move to the previous and next bar, Home goes to the start. Without markers, ← and → move five seconds instead. Keys with Alt, Ctrl or Meta are left to the browser, a held key doesn't toggle playback repeatedly, and the shortcuts are listed in `aria-keyshortcuts`. The native controls keep their own keyboard handling. The current bar is announced politely to screen readers when playback pauses or after a seek, not during continuous playback, where an announcement per bar would drown everything else.

## Rationale

Native elements bring keyboard, focus and assistive-technology support for free and are easy to theme. Restarting, adjusting tempo and choosing whether the page follows are common practice tasks. Keeping those controls together, but moving the settings behind a button on narrow players, makes them accessible without crowding out the seek slider.

## Consequences

- A site that wants other controls (loop, download) builds them in the slot with the public API.
