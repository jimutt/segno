## Context

Segno needs a distinct visual identity for its demo site and examples: a logotype, a typographic system and a page layout that connect music engraving with a modern web tool while keeping the player controls and interface legible. Five directions (classical Urtext, Nordic editorial serif, dark studio console, Italian Didone on a staff, and a Swiss constructivist grid) and three variations of the Swiss grid and logotype were prototyped and compared on the live demo page.

## Decision

- **Logotype (`𝄋egno`):** An inline SVG wordmark where an engraved segno sign (emboldened with a rounded SVG stroke to match the letter stems) serves directly as the initial capital **S** at cap height, paired with neo-grotesk lowercase `egno` in **Archivo** (`500` weight, tight tracking). The S-scroll and diagonal slash use the foreground ink (`currentColor`), and the two flanking repeat dots use the accent colour. The standalone segno mark also serves as the page's SVG favicon.
- **Typography:**
  - Display and headings: **Archivo** (`500` weight in the wordmark, `700` weight for headings).
  - Body copy, disclosures and player controls (inherited from `body`): **IBM Plex Sans** (with `system-ui, sans-serif` fallback), keeping controls and interactive text strictly sans-serif.
  - Status readouts, technical labels and file names: **IBM Plex Mono**.
- **Palette and layout (5C):**
  - Light scheme: cool archival paper (`#f3f4f6`), surface panel (`#e7e9ed`), white sheet (`#ffffff`) and stark ink (`#0d0e0d`), with **Swiss Vermilion** (`#cc291b`) for the logotype's repeat dots, links and `--segno-accent`.
  - Dark scheme (`prefers-color-scheme: dark`): dark ink background (`#101211`), surface (`#181b19`), sheet (`#1f2321`) and light foreground (`#f0f2f0`), with `#ff5c4d` for text links/dots and `#d63022` for `--segno-accent`.
  - Framing: an engraved final-barline top rule (`4px` thick rule above a `1.5px` thin rule) over the header, `1.5px` solid ink borders on the score metadata bar, the `<segno-player>::part(page)` sheet and the own-export disclosure, and `--segno-controls-radius: 0.5rem`.

## Rationale

- The musical segno sign evolved from the letter *S* (*Segno*). Using the engraved sign at cap height as the *S* in *Segno* avoids repeating the initial (*𝄋 Segno*) and avoids inconsistent system-font fallbacks for `U+1D10B`.
- Pairing an authentic engraved segno with bold Swiss neo-grotesk typography and architectural rules reflects Segno's purpose: playing an engraver's fixed pages inside a modern web component.
- Sans-serif body and control typography keeps buttons, timecodes and file pickers crisp at small sizes.

## Consequences

- `demo/index.html` loads `Archivo`, `IBM Plex Sans` and `IBM Plex Mono` from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; `<segno-player>` itself stays font-agnostic and inherits `--segno-font`.
