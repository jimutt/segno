𝄋 Segno

Play a score's own audio on its own engraved pages, and follow along: a marker on the bar being played and a thin cursor that flows from note to note. Built for MuseScore exports.

Try it with your own export

Export a score from MuseScore as SVG, MP3 and the bar positions (.mpos), and optionally the note positions (.spos), as described in the README. Pick all of its files here. They stay in your browser.

Source and documentation: github.com/jimutt/segno. Built from f137c3a.
